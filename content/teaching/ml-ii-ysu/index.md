Graduate-level **Machine Learning II** at **Yerevan State University**,
delivered over two consecutive spring semesters. The course builds on a
first ML course and covers:

- Probabilistic modeling and Bayesian inference
- Gaussian processes and Bayesian optimization
- Modern deep learning (architectures, optimization, generalization)
- Causal inference foundations
- Research reading and project work

Alongside the lectures I supervised student research groups on applied
probabilistic modeling and related topics.
