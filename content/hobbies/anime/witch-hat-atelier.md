The premise does the heavy lifting: magic isn't innate, it's ink —
glyphs drawn with the right hand and the right intent. That single
idea gives the show its texture, from Coco's first clumsy circles to
the ethics of who gets to draw at all. Bug Films renders Kamome
Shirahama's linework lovingly, even if the pacing wobbles in the
middle stretch. Season 2 is already announced.
