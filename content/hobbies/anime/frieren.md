Starts where most fantasy stories end — the demon king is dead, the party
disperses. What follows is a quiet, devastating meditation on how humans
live shorter lives than elves care to notice. Madhouse's direction and
Evan Call's score do a lot of the heavy lifting.

The Mimic episode alone is better comedy than most shows manage in a
full season.
