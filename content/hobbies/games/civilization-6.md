Districts and unstacked cities push you into actually planning a map
instead of optimising one tile. The expansions paper over most of the
base game's rough edges; with Gathering Storm on, late-game diplomacy
finally has teeth.

Still the easiest "I'll just play an hour" trap I've ever fallen into.
