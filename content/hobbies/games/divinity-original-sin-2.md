Larian's combat sandbox is the rare turn-based system that begs to be
broken. Elemental surfaces, teleport chains, polymorph nonsense — every
encounter has a "wait, can I…" solution that almost always works.

The writing punches above its weight too. Fane is one of the best
companions in the genre.
