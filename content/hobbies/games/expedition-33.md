Sandfall's debut leans on the reactive parry/dodge trick to keep
turn-based combat tense without abandoning the genre's bones. The
art direction does a lot of heavy lifting — Belle Époque painting
brought to life — and the soundtrack earns its reputation.

Story sticks the landing in a way most JRPGs only attempt.
