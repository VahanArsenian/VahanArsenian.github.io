GGG keeps adding mechanics faster than I can learn them, and somehow it
mostly works. The build depth is unmatched in the genre — a passive
tree, gem links, ascendancies, and cluster jewels mean two characters
of the same class can play nothing alike.

Currency-based trade is still rough, but the league treadmill keeps
pulling me back every few resets.
